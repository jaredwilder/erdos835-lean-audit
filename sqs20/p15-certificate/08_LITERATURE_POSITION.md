## Disjoint SQS(20)

Etzion and Hartman (SIAM J. Discrete Math. 4 (1991), 182–195) established the classical lower-bound construction giving D(20)≥15. Later disjoint-SQS work continues to cite that bound and improve recursive constructions in other orders.

## Large SQS

Modern papers continue to distinguish near-large families of disjoint SQS from a full nontrivial explicit large set. A full large set at v=20 would require 17 mutually disjoint SQS(20).
